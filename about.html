About

Myths you can wear.

The story

Doric started with my passions. I studied to become an interior advisor at the HMC (Hout- en Meubileringscollege), where I discovered my love for materials and architecture: how stone, structure and form can tell a story.

I have always loved jewelry, but I wanted to make something different. Not just beautiful, but meaningful. So I brought together everything I love: architecture, natural materials and technology.

The name

Doric is the oldest of the Greek architectural orders: the strong, unadorned columns of the temples. That balance between strength and simplicity is the foundation of every piece.

The stones

Every necklace is made from natural stone and named after a figure or place from Greek mythology that shares its character. No two stones are alike, so no two necklaces are either.

Made for you

Each piece is handmade to order. Once you order, your necklace is made just for you.

Its own passport

This is where my love for technology comes in. Every necklace arrives in a Doric box with its own digital passport. Tap the logo with your phone to discover its story, its stones and how to care for it.
